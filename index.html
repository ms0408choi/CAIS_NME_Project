What this simulation can tell you

A higher threshold sends more work to reviewers and lets fewer errors pass unchecked. These scores and outcomes are invented; confidence is not a guarantee of correctness. Human reviewers can also make mistakes. This tool counts errors that bypass review and does not predict errors after human review.

Rule: confidence below the threshold → human review; otherwise → auto-pass. At 0%, all results auto-pass. At 100%, all results go to review.
